# Product scope: Route 66 Challenge

The near-term release is an English Web/PWA, built independently for Kiroween 2026. This virtual walking challenge combines locally tracked steps, milestone postcards and a printable travel passport. It is not navigation or proof of visiting a location.

- Use the editor-curated Illinois chapter, 13 stops from Chicago to the Illinois side of Old Chain of Rocks Bridge. The product distance is 531,084 m, not a survey or directions. Remaining states may be previewed, not presented as playable.
- Credit 700 mm per step by default. An integer millimetre ledger determines unlocks; rounding is for display. The starter badges reward 4,200,000 mm and 12,600,000 mm. North Lawndale, the first collectible stop, is 17,703,000 mm, exactly 25,290 steps at default stride.
- Keep simulated Demo and self-reported Manual independent. Persist progress locally. Same-day Manual corrections replace earlier totals; one-time awards and existing saves are retained. Do not claim a native device connection. Trial behaviour: first badge, second badge, reach stop, save postcard and retain state after refresh.
- The Local Preview has browser print/save for a US Letter portrait passport after the first badge. It excludes original logs and personal identifiers. Doctoring a demo or releasing unlicensed images does not count toward completion.
- Final judging build still needs all 13 story/image sets, completed rights/captions, accessibility, offline behavior, a verified one-page export, contest license, and a short public demo. Hardware health sync is out of scope for this Web entry.
