## Opening reward

At 6,000 steps/day and 0.70 m/step, three days correspond to **12.6 km (~7.8 mi)**. If a sourced landmark does not meet the early reward target, use a separate **First Miles** stamp at that virtual distance. It is a progress badge, not a claim about a landmark or its coordinate. Sort the badge by the measured distance once the chapter alignment is fixed. Aim for a meaningful first story before or with this stamp, not a long empty opening.

## Before promoting this sheet to route data

1. Choose and name the alignment, or document where a clearly labeled schematic connects historic segments. Record the source/version, stop coordinates, distance method and units. Do not interpolate through unverified gaps or treat a generalized line, straight-line estimate, or a historical alternate segment's mileage as a validated cumulative distance.
2. Keep the chapter intro, progress badges, and landmark rewards distinct. Check monotonic distance order and realistic reward intervals. Historical facts may be summarized with linked sources; media needs a separate file-level rights record (creator, source, license, required attribution, and any use restrictions). Do not republish stock maps, agency photos, exhibition graphics, or archival imagery unless the actual file permits the intended distribution; use original renderings as fallback and label illustrations.
3. Prove the unique interaction early. In the signed-out demo, use clearly labeled sample walking to unlock the first stamp or postcard, then print one US Letter passport sheet. It must show only earned entries, readable when actually printed or saved as PDF, with a visible demo label on demo output. Do not wait for chapter completion to enable printing.
